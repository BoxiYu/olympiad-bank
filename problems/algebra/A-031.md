# A-031｜Complex Cubic Inequality

## 题面

Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ be real numbers, with at least one of $a$ or $c$ non-zero, such that
$$
a^2 + ac + c^2 + 1 = b^2 + bd + d^2, \quad \text{and} \quad 2ab + ad + 2cd + bc = 0.
$$
Show that $ac < 0$ or $(3a^2 - b^2 + 1)(3c^2 - d^2 + 1) \le 0$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Notice that $b \neq 0$ or $d \neq 0$ as $a^2 + ac + c^2 + 1 = (a + c/2)^2 + (3/4)c^2 + 1 > 0$. Suppose that $c = 0$. By assumption, we then have $a \neq 0$ and the second equation becomes $2ab + ad = 0$, hence $b = -d/2$. Substituting this into the first equation gives $d^2 = (4/3)(a^2 + 1)$. So $b^2 = (a^2 + 1)/3$ and
$$
(3a^2 - b^2 + 1)(3c^2 - d^2 + 1) = \frac{1}{9}(8a^2 + 2)(-1 - 4a^2) < 0,
$$

$$
(2a + c)b = -(a + 2c)d.
$$
Since $b \neq 0$ or $d \neq 0$, and $(2a + c)(a + 2c) = 2(a + c)^2 + ac > 0$, we deduce that $bd < 0$. Consider the complex numbers $z = a + ib$ and $w = c + id$. Then
$$
z^2 + zw + w^2 = (a^2 - b^2 + ac - bd + c^2 - d^2) + i(2ab + ad + bc + 2cd) = -1.
$$

So
$$
(z^3 + z) - (w^3 + w) = (z - w)(z^2 + zw + w^2 + 1) = 0.
$$
Now
$$
\begin{aligned}
z^3 + z &= a(a^2 - 3b^2 + 1) + ib(3a^2 - b^2 + 1), \\
w^3 + w &= c(c^2 - 3d^2 + 1) + id(3c^2 - d^2 + 1).
\end{aligned}
$$
In particular $d(3c^2 - d^2 + 1) = b(3a^2 - b^2 + 1)$. But $bd < 0$. So
$$
(3a^2 - b^2 + 1)(3c^2 - d^2 + 1) \le 0.
$$

## 提示阶梯

1. 目标中的两个因子很像某个复数多项式的虚部；先把两组实数分别组成复数。
2. 令 z=a+ib、w=c+id，两条已知条件合起来可写成 z²+zw+w²=-1，进而比较 z³+z 与 w³+w。
3. 先由 (2a+c)b=-(a+2c)d 并结合 ac≥0 推出 bd<0；再取 z³+z=w³+w 的虚部，使目标两因子分别与 b、d 相乘后相等。边界情形 c=0 单独代入处理。
