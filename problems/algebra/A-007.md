# A-007｜Polynomial Dilation Quotient

## 题面

Problem:
Find all polynomials $f$ that satisfy the equation
$$
\frac{f(9 x)}{f(3 x)} = \frac{243 x - 729}{x - 81}
$$
for infinitely many values of $x$.

## 答案

f(x) = a x^2 (x - 27)(x - 81)(x - 243) for real constants a

## 解法要点

Solution:
We have
$$
(x-81) f(9 x) = (243 x - 729) f(3 x)
$$
for infinitely many values of $x$. Since both sides of this equation are polynomials, they must then be equal for all $x$.

Plugging in $x=3$, we get $f(27)=0$. Plugging in $x=9$ then gives $f(81)=0$. Plugging in $x=27$ then gives $f(243)=0$. Thus we have that $f(x) = g(x) \cdot (x-27)(x-81)(x-243)$ for some polynomial $g(x)$.

Plugging this into the original equation, we get that for infinitely many $x$,
$$
243 \frac{x-3}{x-81} = \frac{f(9 x)}{f(3 x)} = \frac{g(9 x)}{g(3 x)} \cdot \frac{(9 x-27)(9 x-81)(9 x-243)}{(3 x-27)(3 x-81)(3 x-243)} = \frac{g(9 x)}{g(3 x)} \cdot 27 \frac{x-3}{x-81},
$$
so $\frac{g(9 x)}{g(3 x)} = \frac{243}{27} = 9$ and thus $f$ works if and only if $g(9 x) = 9 g(3 x)$ for all $x$. Thus, the roots of $g(9 x)$ are those of $g(3 x)$, so they must all be zero, so $g$ must be of the form $a x^{n}$. Plugging in $x=1$ then gives $9^{n} = 9 \cdot 3^{n}$, so $n=2$. Indeed, $g(x) = x^{2}$ works.

The solutions are then
$$
f(x) = a x^{2} (x-27)(x-81)(x-243)
$$
for real constants $a$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 关注“对无穷多个 $x$ 成立”对两个多项式意味着什么，并留意缩放后的零点如何传递。
2. 恒等式会给出一串按倍数递进的非零根；提出这些一次因子后，剩余多项式满足一个缩放关系。
3. 依次代入使等式一侧的线性因子为零的 $x$，提出所得三个因子。对剩余的 $g$，由 $g(9x)=9g(3x)$ 先说明其根只能为零，再设 $g(x)=ax^n$ 比较缩放次数。
