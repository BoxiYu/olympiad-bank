# A-009｜Symmetric Quartic System

## 题面

Anna, Anne and Anni seek for real solutions $(x, y)$ to the system of equations
$$
\begin{cases} 4x^3y - x^4 - 3x^2y^2 = 2021, \\ 4y^3x - y^4 - 3y^2x^2 = 2021. \end{cases}
$$
Anna claims that the system of equations has a solution. Anne claims that the system of equations has no solution but at least one of the two equations has solutions. Anni claims that the system of equations has no solution and, even worse, neither of the two equations alone has a solution. Who is right?

## 答案

Anne

## 解法要点

The system does not have a solution since adding the equation gives $-(x - y)^4 = 4042$ whose l.h.s. is non-positive but r.h.s. is positive.
We show that the first equation $4x^3y - x^4 - 3x^2y^2 = 2021$ has solutions (the same could be done for the second equation by symmetry). Dividing the equation by $x^4$ and reordering the terms in the l.h.s. results in
$$
-3 \left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^2 + 4 \left(\frac{y}{x}\right) - 1 = \frac{2021}{x^4}. \qquad (6)
$$
As the discriminant of the quadratic equation $-3t^2 + 4t - 1 = 0$ is positive, there exists a real number $t$ such that $-3t^2 + 4t - 1$ equals a positive number $\varepsilon$. Define $x = \sqrt[4]{\frac{2021}{\varepsilon}}$ and $y = xt$; then $x$ and $y$ satisfy (6) and also the first equation of the given system of equations. Hence Anne is right.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先判断两式能否同时成立，再单独考察其中一式是否可能成立；这两个问题需要不同的组合方式。
2. 两式相加会化成一个四次幂的符号矛盾。考察单个方程时，除以 $x^4$ 并令 $t=y/x$，把问题变为一个二次式能否取正值。
3. 对单个方程取一个使 $-3t^2+4t-1=\varepsilon>0$ 的 $t$，再令 $y=xt$ 并按 $x^4=2021/\varepsilon$ 选取 $x$；结合相加后的符号判断三人的说法。
