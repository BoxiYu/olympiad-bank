# A-040｜Playground Attendance Equation

## 题面

Yesterday in the playground the ratio of boys to girls was $3 : 2$. Today, the number of boys is the square of the number of girls and there are $6$ boys and $7$ girls less than yesterday. How many children were there in the playground yesterday?
(A) 12
(B) 13
(C) 15
(D) 25
(E) 30

## 答案

D

## 解法要点

Yesterday there were $3t$ boys and $2t$ girls in the playground. Today, there are $3t-6$ boys and $2t-7$ girls. From $3t-6 = (2t-7)^2$ we get $4t^2-31t+55 = 0$, so $t_{1,2} = \frac{31\pm\sqrt{81}}{8}$. The only possible solution is $t = 5$, since $t$ is an integer. Yesterday there were $3t = 15$ boys and $2t = 10$ girls in the playground, or $25$ children altogether.

## 提示阶梯

1. 用同一个比例参数表示昨天的男生数和女生数，再据减少人数写出今天的两个人数。
2. 设昨天男、女生分别为 $3t,2t$，由今天男生数等于女生数的平方得到 $3t-6=(2t-7)^2$。
3. 整理并解二次方程 $4t^2-31t+55=0$，再用人数和比例参数必须为整数排除不合条件的根；最后计算 $3t+2t$。
