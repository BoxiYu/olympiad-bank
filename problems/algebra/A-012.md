# A-012｜Exact Trigonometric Expression

## 题面

Problem:

Find the exact value of $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sin 20^{\circ}}-\frac{1}{\cos 20^{\circ}}$.

## 答案

4

## 解法要点

Solution:

Let us denote the expression as $E$:

$$
E = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sin 20^{\circ}} - \frac{1}{\cos 20^{\circ}}
$$

Let us write both terms with a common denominator:

$$
E = \frac{\sqrt{3} \cos 20^{\circ} - 1 \cdot \sin 20^{\circ}}{\sin 20^{\circ} \cos 20^{\circ}}
$$

Recall that $\sin 20^{\circ} \cos 20^{\circ} = \frac{1}{2} \sin 40^{\circ}$.

So,
$$
E = \frac{\sqrt{3} \cos 20^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ}}{\frac{1}{2} \sin 40^{\circ}} = \frac{2 (\sqrt{3} \cos 20^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ})}{\sin 40^{\circ}}
$$

Now, let's try to simplify the numerator:

$\sqrt{3} \cos 20^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ}$

Recall that $a \cos x - b \sin x = R \cos(x + \alpha)$, where $R = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$ and $\tan \alpha = \frac{b}{a}$.

Here, $a = \sqrt{3}$, $b = 1$.

So,
$$
R = \sqrt{(\sqrt{3})^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{3 + 1} = 2
$$

$\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

So $\alpha = 30^{\circ}$.

Therefore,
$$
\sqrt{3} \cos 20^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ} = 2 \cos(20^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) = 2 \cos 50^{\circ}
$$

So,
$$
E = \frac{2 \cdot 2 \cos 50^{\circ}}{\sin 40^{\circ}} = \frac{4 \cos 50^{\circ}}{\sin 40^{\circ}}
$$

But $\cos 50^{\circ} = \sin (90^{\circ} - 50^{\circ}) = \sin 40^{\circ}$.

Therefore,
$$
E = \frac{4 \sin 40^{\circ}}{\sin 40^{\circ}} = 4
$$

**Final Answer:**

$\boxed{4}$

## 提示阶梯

1. 关注正弦、余弦的线性组合与乘积，尝试让分子和分母出现彼此关联的角。
2. 分母用二倍角；分子把 $\sqrt3\cos20^\circ-\sin20^\circ$ 合成为一个移相后的余弦。
3. 将分子写成 $2\cos(20^\circ+30^\circ)$，分母写成 $\tfrac12\sin40^\circ$；最后用余函数关系约去相同的三角量。
