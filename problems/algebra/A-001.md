# A-001｜Birds Between Two Branches

## 题面

There were 25 birds on two branches. After a while 5 birds from the first branch flew over to the second and after a while longer 7 birds from the second flew away. In that moment on the first branch there were twice as many birds than on the second. How many birds were there on each of the two branches in the beginning?

## 答案

Initially 17 birds on the first branch and 8 birds on the second branch.

## 解法要点

When the 7 birds flew away from the second branch the total number of birds on both branches was $25-7=18$. Because in that moment on the first branch there were twice as many birds than on the second, there were $18:3=6$ birds on the second branch, and on the first one were $2 \cdot 6=12$ birds. Hence, before the 7 birds flew away from the second branch, there were $6+7=13$ birds on it. In the beginning, before the 5 birds flew from the first to the second branch, on the second branch were $13-5=8$ birds and on the first were $12+5=17$ birds.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先从鸟飞走后的最后状态倒推，关注此时两根树枝上的总数和倍数关系。
2. 最后状态的总数可由原总数减去飞走的鸟数得到，再按两枝数量之比把它分成三份。
3. 先用 $(25-7)÷3$ 求出较少一枝在最后状态的数量，再依次逆向补回先后发生的两次移动。
