# A-049｜Nonzero Binomial-Difference Coefficients

## 题面

Find all positive integers $n$ such that there exist nonzero real numbers $a, b, c, d$ for which the polynomial
$$
(ax + b)^{1000} - (cx + d)^{1000}
$$
after expanding all brackets (and collecting terms) has exactly $n$ nonzero coefficients.

## 答案

1001, 1000, 500

## 解法要点

The answers are $1001$, $1000$, and $500$.

It is clear that there exist such polynomials with $1001$ and $1000$ nonzero coefficients (for example, $(2x+2)^{1000} - (x+1)^{1000}$ and $(2x+1)^{1000} - (x+1)^{1000}$).

Suppose that in our polynomial there are two coefficients equal to zero — for $x^i$ and $x^j$ ($i > j$). Then $a^i b^{1000-i} = c^i d^{1000-i}$, $a^j b^{1000-j} = c^j d^{1000-j}$; dividing the first equation by the second, we get
$$(\frac{ad}{bc})^i = (\frac{d}{b})^{1000} = (\frac{ad}{bc})^j.$$
Hence $|\frac{ad}{bc}| = 1$, $|\frac{d}{b}| = 1$ and $|\frac{a}{c}| = 1$.

It is clear that replacing $ax+b$ with $(-a)x + (-b)$ does not change our polynomial. Therefore, we may assume $\frac{a}{c} = 1$. Then, if $\frac{b}{d} = 1$, the resulting polynomial $(ax+b)^{1000} - (ax+b)^{1000}$ is identically zero, and if $\frac{b}{d} = -1$, then in the resulting polynomial $(ax+b)^{1000} - (ax - b)^{1000}$, exactly the coefficients at even powers of $x$ vanish, so we get $500$ nonzero coefficients.

## 提示阶梯

1. 研究某次幂项何时消失，而不是逐项展开。若消失项不止一个，任选两个不同次数写出对应系数相等式。
2. 将这两个等式相除，可推出 |ad/(bc)|=1，并进一步得到 |a/c|=|b/d|=1。于是多次消项只可能来自两个一次式之间的符号关系。
3. 同时改变一个一次式的两个系数符号不会改变其偶次幂，可先规范化 a/c 的符号。随后分别检查 b/d 同号与异号：前者发生整体抵消，后者由幂次奇偶性决定哪些项留下；若不足两个零系数，则只剩全不消或仅消一项的情形。
