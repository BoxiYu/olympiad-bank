# C-024｜Three-Pet River Crossing

## 题面

Problem:

A man has three pets: a mouse, a cat, and a dog. If the man leaves the cat and the dog alone, then the dog would kill the cat. If the man leaves the cat and the mouse alone, the cat would eat the mouse. One day the man decided to take his animals to the other side of the river. However, he has a small boat in which he can fit only one of the animals at a time. Show that it is possible for the man to take all of his animals to the other side of the river safely.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:

Let $A$ be the side of the river where the trip starts, and $B$ the other side. In the first trip the man should take the cat to the side $B$. He would leave the mouse and the dog together, but that is not dangerous. Then he will return back for a dog and take it to the side $B$. However when he brings the dog, then he returns the cat from $B$ to $A$. Then he takes the mouse from $A$ to $B$ and comes back to the side $A$ to finally take the cat.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先找出谁不能与另外两只中的任何一只单独留下，它应当成为往返调度的核心。
2. 当下一只动物被送到对岸后，不必空船返回；可以把先前送过河的那只带回，从而解除对岸的危险组合。
3. 先送猫，再送狗；送到狗后立刻把猫带回。此时先处理留在起点的另一只动物，剩下的行程即可自行补全。
