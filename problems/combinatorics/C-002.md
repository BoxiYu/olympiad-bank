# C-002｜Crossing the Seventy-Five Percent Mark

## 题面

Problem:

Aerith and Bob play rounds of pool. At some point Bob had won more rounds than Aerith, but now Aerith has won $85\%$ of their rounds. Show that at some point, Aerith had won exactly $75\%$ of their rounds.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:

At any time, let $a$ and $b$ represent the respective number of rounds won by Aerith and Bob. Now, after $n$ games, $a-3b = n(85\% - 3 \cdot 15\%) > 0$. However, at some point in the past $b$ was greater than $a$, so $a-3b$ was negative. Consider the first subsequent moment when $a-3b$ was non-negative. At that moment,
$$
(a-1)-3b < 0 \leq a-3b,
$$
so as $a$ and $b$ were integers, $a-3b=0$ and $a=75\% \cdot (a+b)$, as desired.
