# C-042｜Colorblind Sock Stopping Probability

## 题面

Problem:

Victor has a drawer with two red socks, two green socks, two blue socks, two magenta socks, two lavender socks, two neon socks, two mauve socks, two wisteria socks, and 2000 copper socks, for a total of 2016 socks. He repeatedly draws two socks at a time from the drawer at random, and stops if the socks are of the same color. However, Victor is red-green colorblind, so he also stops if he sees a red and green sock.
What is the probability that Victor stops with two socks of the same color? Assume Victor returns both socks to the drawer at each step.

## 答案

499752/499753

## 解法要点

Solution:

There are $\binom{2000}{2} + 8\binom{2}{2} = 1999008$ ways to get socks which are matching colors, and four extra ways to get a red-green pair, hence the answer.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先只看一次抽取会触发停止的两类结果：真正同色，以及仅因红绿色盲而被看成同色。
2. 每轮抽后放回且各轮独立，所以最终以哪一类结果停止，只取决于这两类结果在单轮中的计数之比。
3. 真正同色的无序抽法数为 C(2000,2)+8C(2,2)，再把四种红绿配对加入停止情形总数，以前者除以后者。
