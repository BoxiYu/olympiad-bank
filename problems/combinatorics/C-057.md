# C-057｜Positive Suffix Leaders

## 题面

Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{2003}$ be a sequence of real numbers. A term $a_k$, $1 \le k \le 2003$, is said to be a *leading term*, if at least one of the expressions $a_k, a_k+a_{k+1}, \dots, a_k+a_{k+1}+\dots+a_{2003}$ is positive. Prove that the sum of all leading terms is positive provided that the sequence has at least one leading term.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

We solve this problem for any sequence having $n$ terms applying induction with respect to $n$.

The case $n = 1$ is clear.

Suppose that the statement is true for all sequences of length less than $n$.

Now consider a sequence $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$.

*Case 1. $a_1$ is not a leading term.*

Then the set of all leading terms of the sequence $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ coincides with the set of all leading terms of the sequence $a_2, a_3, \dots, a_n$. And by inductive hypothesis we are done.

*Case 2. $a_1$ is a leading term.*

Consider the smallest nonnegative integer $m$, with positive $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_m$. Then the terms $a_2, a_3, \dots, a_m$ are also leading terms and their sum is positive. The sum of all remaining leading terms also is nonnegative by induction hypothesis.

## 提示阶梯

1. 对序列长度归纳，并按首项是否为 leading term 分情况；这样可以把问题转交给删去一段后的短序列。
2. 若首项不是 leading term，直接删去它。若是，取最短的正前缀 $a_1+⋯+a_m>0$，这一整段应当作为一个整体剥离。
3. 由 $m$ 的最小性，前面的部分和都不正，因此对每个 $2≤k≤m$，都有 $a_k+⋯+a_m>0$，这些项全是 leading term；剩余部分再用归纳假设，其贡献非负。
