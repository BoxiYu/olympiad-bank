# C-009｜Two-Coin Posterior Probability

## 题面

Problem:

George has two coins, one of which is fair and the other of which always comes up heads. Jacob takes one of them at random and flips it twice. Given that it came up heads both times, what is the probability that it is the coin that always comes up heads?

## 答案

4/5

## 解法要点

Solution:

Answer: $\frac{4}{5}$.

In general, $P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$, where $P(A|B)$ is the probability of $A$ given $B$ and $P(A \cap B)$ is the probability of $A$ and $B$ (See http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_probability for more information).

If $A$ is the event of selecting the "double-headed" coin and $B$ is the event of Jacob flipping two heads, then $P(A \cap B) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)(1)$, since there is a $\frac{1}{2}$ chance of picking the double-headed coin and Jacob will always flip two heads when he has it.

By conditional probability, $P(B) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)(1) + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{4}\right) = \frac{5}{8}$, so $P(A \mid B) = \frac{1/2}{5/8} = \frac{4}{5}$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 已知连续两次为正面后，两枚硬币不再等可能；比较它们各自产生这份观察的可能性。
2. 设 $A$ 为选中总是正面的硬币，$B$ 为两次均为正面，用 $P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)$。
3. $P(A\cap B)$ 来自“选中该硬币且必出两正面”，而 $P(B)$ 还要加上“选中公平硬币且连出两正面”这一互斥分支；用前者除以后者。
