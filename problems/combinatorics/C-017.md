# C-017｜Tribonacci Square Coefficients

## 题面

Problem:
Let $\frac{1}{1-x-x^{2}-x^{3}}=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty} a_{n} x^{n}$, for what positive integers $n$ does $a_{n-1}=n^{2}$?

## 答案

1, 9

## 解法要点

Solution:
Multiplying both sides by $1-x-x^{2}-x^{3}$ the right hand side becomes $a_{0}+(a_{1}-a_{0}) x+(a_{2}-a_{1}-a_{0}) x^{2}+\ldots$, and setting coefficients of $x^{n}$ equal to each other we find that $a_{0}=1$, $a_{1}=1$, $a_{2}=2$, and $a_{n}=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}$ for $n \geq 3$. Thus the sequence of $a_{n}$'s starts $1,1,2,4,7,13,24,44,81,149,\ldots$. So we now see that $a_{0}=1^{2}$ and $a_{8}=9^{2}$. What makes it impossible for this to happen again is that the sequence is growing exponentially. It will suffice to show that $a_{n}>1.5^{n}$ for $n>2$, since $n^{2}/(n-1)^{2}<1.5$ for $n \geq 6$, thus when $a_{n-1}$ exceeds $n^{2}$ at $n=10$ there can be no more solutions to $a_{n-1}=n^{2}$. Observe that $a_{n}>1.5 a_{n-1}$ for $n=3,4,5$. By way of induction, assume it for $n-2$, $n-1$, and $n$, then $a_{n+1}=a_{n}+a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}>1.5^{n}+1.5^{n-1}+1.5^{n-2}=1.5^{n-2}(1+1.5+1.5^{2})>1.5^{n+1}$. Thus, by induction, $a_{n}>1.5^{n}$ for $n>2$, so the only solutions are $\mathbf{1, 9}$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先由生成函数比较系数得到递推，再把小范围检验与增长速度比较结合起来。
2. 系数满足 $a_0=1,a_1=1,a_2=2$ 以及 $a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}$；证明它最终比相邻平方增长得更快。
3. 先直接检查到序列超过对应平方的位置；随后归纳证明 $a_n>1.5a_{n-1}$，并与 $n^2/(n-1)^2<1.5$ 的范围比较，即可排除之后再次相等。
