# C-061｜Triangle-Free Acquaintance Graph

## 题面

In the company there are no three people who know each other, but for any group of five there are three who have the common acquaintance (not necessarily from that group). Prove that this company can be split into two groups of pairwise unfamiliar people.
(ViktorBogdanskyi)

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

We are going to show that the acquaintances graph has no odd cycles. For the sake of contradiction, let us take the smallest odd cycle. Clearly, there are no edges connecting vertices of this cycle (as it would divide the cycle into two smaller cycles, at least one of which would be odd). Next, let us enumerate people from $1$ to $2k+1$ along the cycle and use the statement for people with numbers $1$, $2$, $3$, $k+2$, $k+3$ (one can easily check that they are pairwise distinct). Note that
there cannot be edges between any three people having the common acquaintance, because it would lead to the existence of a triangle. Hence, if $2k+1=5$, there would be such edges between people $1$, $2$, $3$, $k+2$, $k+3$, so $k>2$.
Then there must be a common acquaintance for $1$, $3$, and one of $k+2$, $k+3$, w.l.o.g. let $1$, $3$, $k+2$ have the common acquaintance.
From here one can easily construct a smaller odd cycle by using the common acquaintance $X$ of $1$ and $k+2$ ($X$ doesn't belong to the original cycle), namely $1$, $2$, $3$, $\ldots$, $k+2$, $X$ and $k+2$, $k+3$, $\ldots$, $2k+1$, $1$, $X$. Both of them are smaller than the original cycle and have lengths of different parity, leading to the contradiction.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把相识关系画成无向图；目标可转化为排除奇环，因此假设存在一条最短奇环。
2. 这条最短奇环没有弦，否则会分出更短的奇环。再从环上挑五个位置使用题设的公共邻点，并利用无三角形限制可能的三人组合。
3. 按环序记顶点为 $1,…,2k+1$，考察 $1,2,3,k+2,k+3$；可迫使 $1,3$ 与后两个中的一个共享环外邻点 $X$。用 $X$ 分别连接 $1$ 和该远端顶点，会得到两条更短且奇偶性不同的环。
