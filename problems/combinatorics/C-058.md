# C-058｜Power-of-Two Subtraction Game

## 题面

Problem:

The numbers $2^{0}, 2^{1}, \cdots, 2^{15}, 2^{16}=65536$ are written on a blackboard. You repeatedly take two numbers on the blackboard, subtract one from the other, erase them both, and write the result of the subtraction on the blackboard. What is the largest possible number that can remain on the blackboard when there is only one number left?

## 答案

131069

## 解法要点

Solution:

Answer: $131069$

If we reverse the order of the numbers in the final subtraction we perform, then the final number will be negated. Thus, the possible final numbers come in pairs with opposite signs. Therefore, the largest possible number is the negative of the smallest possible number.

To get the smallest possible number, clearly we can take the smallest number originally on the board and subtract all of the other numbers from it (you can make this rigorous pretty easily if needed), so the smallest possible number is
$$
1 - \sum_{k=1}^{16} 2^{k} = 1 - 131070 = -131069,
$$
and thus the largest possible number is $131069$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把最终留下的数看成原始各数带正负号的线性组合，并先研究最小可能值。
2. 把最后一次减法的次序颠倒会使最终数变号，所以最大值与最小值互为相反数。
3. 要使结果尽量小，可让原始最小项取正号，而其余每项都取负号；按次序连续相减即可实现这种符号配置。最后利用前述对称性收尾。
