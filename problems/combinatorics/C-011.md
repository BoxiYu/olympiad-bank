# C-011｜Restricted-Difference Pairings

## 题面

Consider the set $X=\{1,2,3, \ldots, 2018\}$. How many positive integers $k$ with $2 \leq k \leq 2017$ that satisfy the following conditions:

i) There exists some partition of the set $X$ into $1009$ disjoint pairs which are $(a_{1}, b_{1}), (a_{2}, b_{2}), \ldots, (a_{1009}, b_{1009})$ with $|a_{i}-b_{i}| \in \{1, k\}$.

ii) For all partitions satisfy the condition (i), the sum $T=\sum_{i=1}^{1009}|a_{i}-b_{i}|$ has the right most digit is $9$.

## 答案

403

## 解法要点

First, we notice that for all $2 \leq k \leq 2017$, we always can find a partition satisfy i). For example, we can choose $a_{i}=2i-1$, $b_{i}=2i$ with $i=1,2, \ldots, 1009$.

Denote $m$ as the number of pairs $(a_{i}, b_{i})$ with $|a_{i}-b_{i}|=k$ then
$$
T=(1009-m) \cdot 1 + m \cdot k = 1009 + (k-1)m.
$$
Hence, the necessary condition of $k$ is $(k-1)m$ is divisible by $10$. First, we can see that all numbers $k \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$ satisfy the given condition.

Indeed, we have some cases:

1. If $k \equiv 1 \pmod{10}$ then $10 \mid k-1$ and ii) is satisfied.

2. If $k \equiv 6 \pmod{10}$ then $k$ is even. Thus, we need to prove $m$ is even. This is true because we can write $m = m_{1} + m_{2}$ with $m_{1}, m_{2}$ are the number of pairs with the difference is $k$ that have same parity and different parity. In $X$, the number of even and odd numbers are equal and all the pairs $(a_{i}, b_{i})$ with $|a_{i}-b_{i}|=1$ also contain exactly $1$ number in each type of even/odd. These imply that $m_{1}=m_{2}$ or $m$ is an even number.

Hence, all numbers $k \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$ satisfy the given condition.

Next, we will prove that all other numbers $k$ do not satisfy the given condition.

3. If $k$ is odd then $k-1$ is not divisible by $5$. We choose the partition with $a_{1}=1$, $b_{1}=k+1$ and the rest are divided into pairs with $a_{i}, b_{i}$ being two consecutive numbers. It is easy to check that this partition satisfies i) but not ii) because $T=1008+k \not\equiv 9 \pmod{10}$.

4. If $k$ is even, $k-1$ is not divisible by $5$ then $k-1$ is an odd number that is not divisible by $5$. We choose the partition with exactly two pairs $(1, k+1), (2, k+2)$ and the rest are divided into pairs with $a_{i}, b_{i}$ being two consecutive numbers. Similarly with the previous case, we have $T=1009+2(k-1) \not\equiv 9 \pmod{10}$.

Therefore, the necessary and sufficient condition of $k$ is $k \equiv 1 \pmod{5}$. From $2$ to $2017$, we have $403$ numbers in total satisfy it.

## 提示阶梯

1. 不要直接枚举配对；记录差为 $k$ 的配对数，先把 $T$ 的个位条件化成关于这个计数的同余条件。
2. 若差为 $k$ 的配对有 $m$ 个，则 $T=1009+(k-1)m$。再按 $k$ 的奇偶性分析 $m$，并为不满足的余数类构造反例配对。
3. 当 $k$ 为偶数且需控制额外的因子 $2$ 时，用集合中奇偶数一样多及差为 1 的配对一奇一偶推出 $m$ 为偶数；排除其余情形时，奇 $k$ 放一个差为 $k$ 的配对，偶 $k$ 放两个，其余全配相邻数。
