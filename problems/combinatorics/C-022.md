# C-022｜Binary Complement Race

## 题面

Two players, Abigail and Zoe, play a game. Abigail chooses an odd number less than $800$ and writes it on the blackboard. Zoe now chooses a different odd number less than $1200$ and writes it on the blackboard. Each in turn replaces their number by the difference between their number and the smallest power of $2$ bigger than their number. If both players reach $1$ on the same move the game is drawn. If not, the player who first reaches $1$ loses.
Who has the winning strategy?

## 答案

Zoe

## 解法要点

Let $n$ be the current number of one of the players. Because both players start with an odd number, $n$ will be odd as it remains positive throughout the game. If $n = \sum_{i=0}^{k} b_i 2^i$ with $b_i \in \{0, 1\}$ is the binary representation of $n$, then $b_0 = 1$. If we assume $b_k = 1$, then the smallest power of $2$ that is bigger than $n$ is $2^{k+1}$, hence, the next number for this player will be
$$
2^{k+1} - n = 1 + \sum_{i=0}^{k} 2^i - \sum_{i=0}^{k} b_i 2^i = 1 + \sum_{i=0}^{k} (1 - b_i) 2^i = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} c_i 2^i,
$$
where $c_i = 1 - b_i$ for $1 \le i < k$ and $c_0 = 1$. More visually, the effect of one turn is
$$
(1 b_{k-1} b_{k-2} \cdots b_2 b_1 1)^2 \mapsto (\overline{b_{k-1} b_{k-2} \cdots b_2 b_1} 1)^2
$$
where $\overline{0} = 1$ and $\overline{1} = 0$. This new number has at most $k$ binary digits, but it may have fewer if $b_{k-1}$ was equal to $1$.
In general, all the leading digits $1$ will be lost, i.e. if $b_k = b_{k-1} = \dots = b_{k-j+1} = 1$ and $b_{k-j} = 0$ then the new number will have $j$ digits less than $n$. Therefore, the number of turns to reach $1$ is equal to the number of blocks of consecutive equal digits in the binary representation of the initial number, where we ignore the last (units) digit in the count.
For example, the smallest number which allows for ten turns is
$$
1365 = (10101010101)^2
$$
---
The smallest number that allows for nine turns is $683 = (1010101011)^2$.
The number $1195 = (10010101011)^2$ allows for nine turns as well.
Abigail can choose $683$ which enables her to carry out nine steps. If Zoe chooses $1195$ she will also be able to carry out nine steps and so she will win.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把每次变换放到二进制中观察，重点追踪数位长度和相邻数位的变化。
2. 一次变换会删去开头连续的若干个 $1$，并翻转其后的中间数位；到达 $1$ 的步数可由忽略末位后的连续同色块数刻画。
3. 先找出限制内能走九步的最小奇数，再让后选者挑一个不同且同样有九个数位块的奇数；比较两人同时到达 $1$ 时的胜负规则即可收尾。
