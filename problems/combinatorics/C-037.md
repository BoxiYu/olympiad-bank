# C-037｜Maximum Distance from Squares

## 题面

Problem:

Alice starts with the number $0$. She can apply $100$ operations on her number. In each operation, she can either add $1$ to her number, or square her number. After applying all operations, her score is the minimum distance from her number to any perfect square. What is the maximum score she can attain?

## 答案

94

## 解法要点

Solution:

Answer: $94$

Note that after applying the squaring operation, Alice's number will be a perfect square, so she can maximize her score by having a large number of adding operations at the end. However, her score needs to be large enough that the many additions do not bring her close to a larger square. Hence the strategy is as follows: $2$ additions to get to $2$, $4$ consecutive squares to get to $65536$, and $94$ more additions for a score of $94$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 最后一次平方之后得到的是完全平方数，所以得分只取决于末尾连续做了多少次加法，以及是否靠近下一个平方数。
2. 应把尽量多的加法留在末尾，同时让平方阶段结束时的数足够大，使这些加法不会越过相邻平方数的中点。
3. 先用两次加法到达 2，再连续平方四次；其余操作全部加 1，并将终值到前后两个平方数的距离作比较。
