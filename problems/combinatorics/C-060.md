# C-060｜Odd-Incidence Polygon Triangulation

## 题面

Is it possible to subdivide a convex $2014$-gon, by means of diagonals that do not intersect, into triangles, in such a way that each vertex be incident to an odd number of triangles?

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

No, it is not possible. First, note that each triangulated polygon can be bicoloured: The triangles may each be assigned either of two colours, black and white, in such a way that the colours alternate (i.e. adjacent triangles have opposite colours). This is easily shown inductively, since a triangulated polygon can be cut into two smaller ones across a diagonal.

It is clear that a triangulated polygon has an odd number of triangles incident at each vertex if and only if its edges are uniformly coloured in a single colour.

We now prove that, if $n$ is not divisible by $3$ (which $2014$ clearly isn't), there exists no triangulated $n$-gon having an odd number of triangles incident at each vertex. For, towards a contradiction, consider the smallest such $n$-gon, and, without loss of generality, suppose its edges to be black.

We first assume there exists a diagonal cutting the $n$-gon into two smaller polygons $P$ and $Q$, neither of which is a triangle. Say the two pieces have $p$ and $q$ vertices, respectively, where $p, q \ge 4$ and $p+q-2=n$.

Since the two triangles adjacent at the cutting line have opposing colours, one of the pieces, say $P$, will have received a black edge at the cut, and so will still have all its edges coloured black. The other, $Q$, will have a white edge exposed at the cut line, to which we now adjoin a black triangle. Call this augmented polygon $Q'$. Both $P$ and $Q'$ will then be bicoloured with black edges. Since $P$ and $Q'$ have $p < n$ and $q + 1 < n$ vertices, respectively, our hypothesis on the minimality of $n$ may be applied to show that both numbers $p$ and $q + 1$ are divisible by $3$. But then so is $n = p + (q + 1) - 3$.

Hence all diagonals of the $n$-gon cut off a triangle. This can only happen if $n \le 6$, and it is easy to verify that a square and a pentagon cannot be triangulated in the manner stipulated.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先给三角剖分作黑白二染色，相邻三角形异色；把每个顶点关联的三角形数转译成边界边的颜色条件。
2. 各顶点关联数为奇数，恰好对应所有边界边同色。假设存在边数不被 $3$ 整除的最小反例，沿一条两侧都不是三角形的对角线切开。
3. 切开的两块中，一块保留同色边界；另一块在切口补一个相反颜色的三角形后也有同色边界。最小性迫使两块的边数满足模 $3$ 条件，合并后与原边数矛盾；于是只需处理所有对角线都切下三角形的几个小多边形。
