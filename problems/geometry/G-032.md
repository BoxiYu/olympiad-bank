# G-032｜Opposite Supporting Vertices

## 题面

Problem:

Let $A_{1} A_{2} \cdots A_{2 n}$ be a convex $2 n$-gon. Prove that there is an $i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n$) and a pair of parallel lines, each intersecting the $2 n$-gon only once, one at $A_{i}$ and one at $A_{n+i}$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:

Consider the "highest" and "lowest" points of the $2 n$-gon, with respect to any chosen orientation. Unless a side of the $2 n$-gon is perfectly horizontal, these points will be unique and thus a horizontal line through them will not meet the $2 n$-gon again. These two points will also be vertices of the $2 n$-gon; call them $A_{i}$ and $A_{j}$ respectively. Say that the imbalance in this situation is $i-j \bmod 2 n$, which (being nonzero) we can think of as an integer from $1$ to $2 n-1$ inclusive. We would like to prove that the $2 n$-gon can be rotated such that the imbalance is $n$.

Consider smoothly rotating the $2 n$-gon counterclockwise (assume that the vertices are labeled $A_{1}, \ldots, A_{2 n}$ in counterclockwise order). Note that the imbalance does not change unless one of the sides becomes momentarily horizontal. If the top side becomes horizontal, it goes down by $1$; if the bottom side becomes horizontal, it goes up by $1$; and if both sides momentarily become horizontal, it does not change at all. But after rotating by $180^{\circ}$, the top and bottom vertices are switched: if the imbalance was between $0$ and $n$, it is now between $n$ and $2 n$ and vice versa. Since the imbalance can never be $0$ and never jumps by more than $1$, it must be $n$ at some point.

## 提示阶梯

1. 选定一个方向，考察多边形在该方向上的唯一最高、最低顶点；让这个方向连续旋转并追踪两顶点的编号差。
2. 把最高与最低顶点的编号差模 $2n$ 定义为“不平衡量”。它只在某条边瞬间变为水平时改变，而且每次改变幅度至多为一个单位。
3. 将方向旋转半周后，最高、最低顶点交换，因此不平衡量从区间的一侧移到另一侧。结合它从不取零且不能跨越目标值，找到编号恰相差半圈的时刻。
