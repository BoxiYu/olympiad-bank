# G-061｜Circumcenter Triangles and Euler-Line Concurrency

## 题面

Given acute-angled triangle $ABC$. $O$ is the circumcenter, $H$ is the orthocenter and $AH_A$, $BH_B$, $CH_C$ are the altitudes of $\triangle ABC$. Denote by $A_1$, $B_1$, $C_1$ the circumcenters of triangles $BOC$, $COA$ and $AOB$ respectively. Prove that the lines $A_1H_A$, $B_1H_B$, $C_1H_C$ meet at a point, which lies on the Euler line of $\triangle ABC$. (Euler line of $\triangle ABC$ is the line passing through the circumcenter and centroid of a triangle).

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

It is easy to see that point $O$ is the incenter of $\triangle A_1B_1C_1$, since $OC$ is perpendicular to the tangent of the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ at the point $C$ and is parallel to $H_AH_B$.

Therefore $OC \perp H_A H_B \Rightarrow A_1 B_1 \parallel H_A H_B$. And so $\Delta A_1 B_1 C_1 \sim \Delta H_A H_B H_C$ (Fig.29).

Hence $A_1 H_A \cap B_1 H_B \cap C_1 H_C = X$, where point $X$ is the homothety center of these triangles. Since $O$ is the incenter of $\Delta A_1 B_1 C_1$, and $H$ is the incenter of $\Delta H_A H_B H_C$, then $X \in OH$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 比较 $\triangle A_1B_1C_1$ 与垂足三角形 $\triangle H_AH_BH_C$ 的对应边方向；若能找出位似，再追踪两个三角形中的特殊点。
2. $OC$ 垂直于 $H_AH_B$，而 $A_1B_1$ 与相应切线方向一致，因此 $A_1B_1\parallel H_AH_B$；循环处理其余两边，可得到两三角形相似且对应边平行。
3. 令 $X=A_1H_A\cap B_1H_B$，用上述位似关系检查第三对对应顶点的连线也经过 $X$。最后追踪位似下两三角形内心 $O$ 与 $H$ 的对应位置。
