# G-035｜Angles of Pythagorean Triangles

## 题面

Let $\alpha$ be the radian measure of the smallest angle in a $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle. Let $\beta$ be the radian measure of the smallest angle in a $7$-$24$-$25$ right triangle. In terms of $\alpha$, what is $\beta$?
(A) $\frac{\alpha}{3}$ (B) $\alpha - \frac{\pi}{8}$ (C) $\frac{\pi}{2} - 2\alpha$ (D) $\frac{\alpha}{2}$ (E) $\pi - 4\alpha$

## 答案

C

## 解法要点

Because $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the smallest angles in these triangles, $\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$, $\cos \alpha = \frac{4}{5}$, $\sin \beta = \frac{7}{25}$, and $\cos \beta = \frac{24}{25}$. By a Double Angle Formula,
$$
\sin(2\alpha) = 2 \sin \alpha \cdot \cos \alpha = 2 \cdot \frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{24}{25} = \cos \beta = \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \beta\right).
$$
Because both $2\alpha$ and $\beta$ are acute, $2\alpha = \frac{\pi}{2} - \beta$, so $\beta = \frac{\pi}{2} - 2\alpha$.


Using complex numbers in polar form, $4 + 3i = 5(\cos \alpha + i \sin \alpha)$. Squaring gives $7 + 24i = 25(\cos \alpha + i \sin \alpha)^2$. Similarly, $24 + 7i = 25(\cos \beta + i \sin \beta)$. Multiplying these two equations yields
$$
(7 + 24i)(24 + 7i) = 25(\cos \alpha + i \sin \alpha)^2 \cdot 25(\cos \beta + i \sin \beta) \\
625i = 625 (\cos(2\alpha + \beta) + i \sin(2\alpha + \beta)) \\
\cos \frac{\pi}{2} + i \sin \frac{\pi}{2} = \cos(2\alpha + \beta) + i \sin(2\alpha + \beta).
$$
Because both $2\alpha$ and $\beta$ are acute, $2\alpha + \beta = \frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\beta = \frac{\pi}{2} - 2\alpha$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把两个最小锐角的正弦、余弦分别由直角三角形边长写出，寻找其中由倍角产生的对应。
2. 对 $\alpha$ 使用正弦倍角公式；所得值恰好能与 $\beta$ 的余弦对应，再利用余函数关系连接两个角。
3. 写成 $\sin(2\alpha)=\cos\beta=\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}-\beta)$。注意核对所涉及角的取值范围，以排除正弦相等产生的其他分支，再自行整理。
