# G-035｜Incircle Contact-Line Identities

## 题面

Consider a triangle $ABC$, such that $\angle B = 90^\circ$. Denote by $I$ the in-center and let $F$, $D$ and $E$ be the points where the incircle touches sides $[AB]$, $[BC]$, and $[AC]$ respectively. If $CI \cap EF = \{M\}$ and $DM \cap AB = \{N\}$, show that:

a) $AI = ND$;

b) $FM = \frac{EI \cdot EM}{EC}$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

a. Triangle $AFE$ is isosceles with $AE = AF$, and $AI \perp FE$, hence $\angle AEF = 90^\circ - \angle A/2$. In the same way from the isosceles triangle $CDE$ we get $\angle DEC = 90^\circ - \angle C/2$. As a consequence $\overline{MED} = 180^\circ - \overline{AEF} - \overline{DEC} = 180^\circ - (180^\circ - (\hat{A} + \hat{C})/2) = 45^\circ$. (*)

As $\triangle MDC \equiv \triangle MEC$, we obtain $MD = ME$ (**). By (*) and (**), the triangle $\triangle MED$ is right angled and isosceles. As a consequence $DN \perp EF$ and, because $AI \perp EF$, we obtain $DN \parallel AI$. As $AN \parallel ID$ we conclude that the quadrilateral $ANDI$ is a parallelogram, so $AI = ND$.

b. We have $\overline{EFD} = 180^\circ - \overline{AFE} - \overline{BFD} = (\hat{A} + \hat{B})/2 = 90^\circ - \hat{C}/2 = \overline{DIC}$ so $\triangle FMD \sim \triangle IDC$. We conclude $\frac{FM}{ID} = \frac{MD}{DC}$ which implies
$$
FM = \frac{ID \cdot MD}{DC} = \frac{EI \cdot EM}{EC}.
$$

## 提示阶梯

1. 先用接触弦 EF 与两个角平分线整理角度，寻找经过 D、M 的等腰直角结构；第二问则应把 FM 放进一对相似三角形。
2. 由△AFE、△CDE等腰可算得∠MED=45°，而△MDC≌△MEC给出 MD=ME，所以△MED为等腰直角三角形。另由角度关系可得到△FMD∽△IDC。
3. 等腰直角结构给出 DN⊥EF，而 AI⊥EF；再结合 AN∥ID，第一问由平行四边形的对边收尾。第二问从相似比例 FM/ID=MD/DC 出发，用同圆半径与相切线段对应替换 ID、MD、DC 即可。
