# G-052｜Nested Incircles and Chord Midpoint

## 题面

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $m(\angle B) = 90^\circ$. The incircle of $\triangle ABC$ is tangent to the side $BC$ at point $D$. Let the intersection of lines $XZ$ and $AD$ be $K$, where $X$ and $Z$ are the centers of incircles of $\triangle ABD$ and $\triangle ADC$, respectively. $XZ$ intersects the circumscribed circle of $\triangle ABC$ at $U$ and $V$ and the midpoint of segment $[UV]$ is $M$. Let $Y$ be the point of intersection of the line $AD$ with the circumscribed circle of $\triangle ABC$ different from $A$. Prove that $|CY| = 2|MK|$. (Cafer Tayyar Yıldırım).

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Let the incircle of $\triangle ABD$ be tangent to $BC$ at $T$ and the incircle of $\triangle ADC$ be tangent to $BC$ at $S$. The radii of incircles of $\triangle ABD$ and $\triangle ADC$ are $r_1$ and $r_2$, respectively. Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
DS &= \frac{AD + DC - AC}{2} = \frac{AD + BC - BD - AC}{2} \\
&= \frac{AD - BD - AB + AB + BC - AC}{2} \\
&= \frac{AB + BC - AC}{2} - \frac{AB + BD - AD}{2} \\
&= BD - BT = TD.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $X$ and $Z$ are centers of incircles, $XD \perp DZ$. Therefore,
$$
XZ^2 = XD^2 + DZ^2 = r_1^2 + TD^2 + r_2^2 + TD^2.
$$
and since
$$
XZ^2 = (r_1 - r_2)^2 + TS^2 = (r_1 - r_2)^2 + 4TD^2
$$
we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_1^2 + r_2^2 + 2TD^2 &= (r_1 - r_2)^2 + 4TD^2 \\
2r_1r_2 &= 2TD^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus,
$$
\begin{aligned}
XZ^2 &= r_1^2 + r_2^2 + 2TD^2 = (r_1 + r_2)^2 \\
XZ &= r_1 + r_2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Obviously, $XK \ge r_1$ and $ZK \ge r_2$. Since $XK + ZK = r_1 + r_2$, $XK = r_1$, $ZK = r_2$ and $XZ \perp AD$. Let $O$ be the center of circumscribed circle of $\triangle ABC$ (the midpoint of the segment $AC$). Then $OM \perp UV$. Since $AC$ is a radius $CY \perp AY$. Thus, $UV$ and $CY$ are parallel. Suppose $OM \cap CY = N$. Then $ON \perp CY$ and $YN = NC$. In the rectangle $KYNM$, $MK = NY$. Therefore, $CY = 2MK$. Done.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先研究两个小三角形的内心连线 XZ 与 AD 的位置关系，再把圆中弦 UV 的中点性质接进来。前半段应只依赖切线段和两个小内切圆半径。
2. 设两小内切圆在 BC 上的切点为 T、S，半径为 r₁、r₂；切线段计算给出 DS=DT。分别从直角分解和横纵差写 XZ²，可推出 XZ=r₁+r₂，进而迫使 XK=r₁、ZK=r₂ 且 XZ⊥AD。
3. 设外心为 O，则 O 是 AC 中点且 OM⊥UV；又因 CY⊥AY，可得 UV∥CY。令 OM 与 CY 交于 N，证明 KYNM 为矩形并注意 N 是 CY 的中点，此时所求长度关系只差把半段还原成整段。
