# G-065｜Circumcenter Isosceles Trapezoid

## 题面

In an acute triangle $ABC$, let $D$ be point on the side $[BC]$ different than the vertices. Let $M_1, M_2, M_3, M_4, M_5$ be the midpoints of the line segments $[AD], [AB], [AC], [BD], [CD]$, respectively; $O_1, O_2, O_3, O_4$ be the circumcenters of the triangles $ABD, ACD, M_1M_2M_4, M_1M_3M_5$, respectively; $S$ and $T$ be the midpoints of the line segments $AO_1$ and $AO_2$, respectively. Prove that $SO_3O_4T$ is an isosceles trapezoid.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

As $\angle O_1M_1A = \angle O_1M_2A = 90^\circ$, we have that $O_1M_1AM_2$ is a cyclic quadrilateral and the point $S$ is its circumcenter. Hence $S$ is the circumcenter of the triangle $AM_1M_2$.

Next we observe that the triangles $AM_1M_2$ and $M_4M_2M_1$ are congruent since $AM_1 = M_4M_2 = \frac{AD}{2}$ and $AM_2 = M_4M_1 = \frac{AB}{2}$. Therefore we obtain that the quadrilateral $SM_1O_3M_2$ is a rhombus and hence the line $M_1M_2$ is the perpendicular bisector of the line segment $[SO_3]$.

Similarly we can get that $M_1M_3$ is the perpendicular bisector of the line segment $[TO_1]$. As the points $M_1, M_2$ and $M_3$ are collinear, the result follows.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先寻找 S、T 的另一种身份：它们分别是由 A 和相邻两个中点组成的三角形的外心。这样可把它们与 O₃、O₄放进同一组全等关系中。
2. 由 O₁M₁⊥AM₁、O₁M₂⊥AM₂，四边形 O₁M₁AM₂ 共圆且 S 是其圆心。再比较 △AM₁M₂ 与 △M₄M₂M₁，可得 SM₁O₃M₂ 为菱形。
3. 因此直线 M₁M₂ 是 SO₃ 的垂直平分线；同理，M₁M₃ 是 TO₄ 的垂直平分线。注意 M₁、M₂、M₃ 共线，把 S、T 关于这条直线反射后分别得到 O₃、O₄，即可读出所需的平行与等长关系。
