# G-014｜Square Side-Point Orthocenter

## 题面

On the sides $AB$ and $BC$ of the square $ABCD$ we marked points $K$ and $L$ correspondingly so that $KB = LC$. Segments $AL$ and $CK$ intersect at point $P$. Prove that lines $DP$ and $KL$ are perpendicular.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

By construction $\Delta ADK = \Delta BAL$. Then $\angle BAL + \angle ALB = \angle BAL + \angle AKD = 90^\circ$, and $\Delta AMK$ has a right angle $M$, so $AL \perp DK$ (fig. 14).

Similarly $DL \perp CK$. Then lines $AL$ and $CK$, which intersect at point $P$, contain two altitudes of the $\Delta DKL$, and therefore the line $DP$ has to contain the third altitude, so $KL \perp DP$, as desired.

## 提示阶梯

1. 尝试把 $P$ 识别为某个三角形的垂心；应围绕由 $D,K,L$ 组成的三角形寻找两条高。
2. 由正方形边长和 $KB=LC$ 可比较 $\triangle ADK$ 与 $\triangle BAL$，从对应角与直角关系推出 $AL\perp DK$；另一组同理。
3. 再得到 $CK\perp DL$。由于 $P=AL\cap CK$，这两条线已经是 $\triangle DKL$ 的两条高，识别出 $P$ 的身份后考察从第三个顶点引出的线即可。
