# G-043｜Integer-Centered Sphere Through Three Points

## 题面

Problem:
A sphere is centered at a point with integer coordinates and passes through the three points $(2,0,0)$, $(0,4,0)$, $(0,0,6)$, but not the origin $(0,0,0)$. If $r$ is the smallest possible radius of the sphere, compute $r^{2}$.

## 答案

51

## 解法要点

Solution: Let $(x, y, z)$ be the center of the sphere. By the given condition, we have
$$
(x-2)^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} = x^{2} + (y-4)^{2} + z^{2} = x^{2} + y^{2} + (z-6)^{2}.
$$
Subtracting $x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2}$ yields
$$
x^{2} - (x-2)^{2} = y^{2} - (y-4)^{2} = z^{2} - (z-6)^{2},
$$
or
$$
4(x-1) = 8(y-2) = 12(z-3)
$$
Therefore $(x-1, y-2, z-3)$ must be $(6t, 3t, 2t)$ for some integer $t$. Checking small values of $t$ yields that the possibilities for $(x, y, z)$ closest to $(2,0,0)$ are $(-5,-1,1)$, $(1,2,3)$, and $(7,5,5)$. The second yields a sphere that passes through the origin and is thus forbidden. The other two yield $r^{2} = 51$ and $r^{2} = 75$, so 51 is the answer.

## 提示阶梯

1. 设球心为整数坐标点，利用它到三个已知点距离相等，把三个二次式相减以消去平方项。
2. 相减后得到 4(x-1)=8(y-2)=12(z-3)，所以整数球心落在一条带整数参数的直线上。
3. 写成 (x-1,y-2,z-3)=(6t,3t,2t)，把到任一已知点的距离平方写成 t 的二次式；排除使球经过原点的参数，再比较邻近整数参数。
