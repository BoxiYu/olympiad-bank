# G-058｜Angle Bisector from Intersecting Circles

## 题面

We are given a right-angled triangle $MNP$ with right angle in $P$. Let $k_M$ be the circle with center $M$ and radius $MP$, and let $k_N$ be the circle with center $N$ and radius $NP$.
Let $A$ and $B$ be the common points of $k_M$ and the line $MN$, and let $C$ and $D$ be the common points of $k_N$ and the line $MN$, with $C$ between $A$ and $B$.
Prove that the line $PC$ bisects the angle $APB$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Let $\alpha = \angle NMP$ and $\beta = \angle MNP$. Because the sum of angles in $MNP$ is $180^\circ$, we obtain $\alpha + \beta = 90^\circ$. Since $BP$ is a chord of the circle through $P$ and with mid-point $M$, we have
$$
\angle BAP = \frac{1}{2} \angle BMP = \frac{1}{2} \alpha.
$$
Similarly, for the chord $CP$ in the circle $k_N$, we obtain
$$
\angle CDP = \frac{1}{2} \angle CNP = \frac{1}{2} \beta.
$$

Since $NP$ and $MP$ are perpendicular, $NP$ is a tangent of the circle $k_M$ and $MP$ is a tangent of the circle $k_N$. Considering the chord $BP$ in the circle $k_M$, we therefore have
$$
\angle BPN = \angle BAP = \frac{1}{2}\alpha
$$
and with the chord $CP$ in $k_N$ we have
$$
\angle MPC = \angle CDP = \frac{1}{2}\beta
$$
It therefore follows that
$$
\angle CPB = \angle MPN - \angle BPN - \angle MPC = 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\alpha - \frac{1}{2}\beta = 45^\circ,
$$
and since $\angle APB = 90^\circ$ we also have
$$
\angle APC = \angle APB - \angle CPB = 45^\circ
$$
It therefore follows that $PC$ bisects the angle $APB$ as claimed. $\square$

## 提示阶梯

1. 把原直角三角形的两个锐角记下，尝试用两个圆中的弦角和切线角来拆分点 $P$ 附近的角。
2. 设 $\alpha=\angle NMP,\beta=\angle MNP$，则 $\alpha+\beta=90^\circ$。弦所对的角与切弦关系分别给出 $\angle BPN=\alpha/2$、$\angle MPC=\beta/2$。
3. 按射线次序写出 $\angle CPB=\angle MPN-\angle BPN-\angle MPC$ 并代入上面的半角关系；随后再由以 $AB$ 为直径的圆确定 $\angle APB$。
