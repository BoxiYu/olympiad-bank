# G-030｜Altitude-Foot Reflection

## 题面

In an acute triangle $ABC$, the altitudes $AA_1$, $BB_1$, $CC_1$ are drawn. A perpendicular to $AC$ passing through $A_1$ intersects line $B_1C_1$ at $D$. Prove that $\angle ADC = 90^\circ$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Let $K$ be the intersection point of segments $A_1D$ and $AC$ (see Figure 5).

From the equality $\angle AB_1B = \angle AA_1B = 90^\circ$, it follows that the quadrilateral $AB_1A_1B$ is inscribed in a circle (with diameter $AB$), so $\angle A_1B_1K = 180^\circ - \angle A_1B_1A = \angle ABC$.

Similarly, the quadrilateral $BC_1B_1C$ is inscribed in a circle (with diameter $BC$); therefore, $\angle DB_1K = 180^\circ - \angle C_1B_1C = \angle ABC$.

We obtain that $\angle A_1B_1K = \angle DB_1K$. From the condition, $\angle A_1KB_1 = \angle DKB_1 = 90^\circ$; hence, the right triangles $A_1B_1K$ and $DB_1K$ are congruent (by a leg and an acute angle), whence $A_1K = DK$.

The last equality means that points $A_1$ and $D$ are symmetric with respect to the line $AC$; therefore, triangles $A_1AC$ and $DAC$ are symmetric with respect to this line. Then $\angle ADC = \angle AA_1C = 90^\circ$, as required.

## 提示阶梯

1. 令 A₁D 与 AC 交于 K，尝试证明 A₁ 与 D 关于 AC 对称。为此可比较直角三角形 A₁B₁K 和 DB₁K。
2. 由 AB₁A₁B、BC₁B₁C 分别共圆，可把 ∠A₁B₁K 与 ∠DB₁K 都转化为 ∠ABC；同时两三角形在 K 处均为直角。
3. 两直角三角形共用 B₁K，结合相等锐角可得全等，因此 A₁K=DK。由 K 在 AC 上可认出 AC 是 A₁D 的垂直平分线，再把目标角对应到高 AA₁ 与 BC 所成的角。
