# G-059｜Largest Vertex-Passing Tangent Circle

## 题面

Consider a right angled triangle $ABC$ with sides of length $3$, $4$, and $5$. Determine the greatest possible radius of a circle that is tangent to two among the lines $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$ and that in addition passes through at least one of the points $A$, $B$, and $C$.

## 答案

15

## 解法要点

Consider a general triangle $ABC$. Suppose we have a circle that touches the lines $AB$ and $AC$. Since it cannot also pass through the point $A$, we may suppose it passes through the point $C$. The centre of the circle will then lie either on the internal, or the external, bisector of the angle at $A$.

Assume the centre of the circle lies on the internal bisector. Then its radius is
$$
r = b \tan \frac{A}{2} = 2R \sin B \tan \frac{A}{2},
$$
where $R$ denotes the circumradius. The maximal radius is obtained when $A \ge B \ge C$ (the expression $\frac{\sin x}{\tan \frac{x}{2}} = 2 \cos^2 \frac{x}{2}$ is strictly decreasing for $0 \le x \le 180^\circ$).

Assume now the centre of the circle lies on the external bisector. Then its radius is
$$
s = b \tan \frac{B+C}{2} = \frac{2R \sin B}{\tan \frac{A}{2}}.
$$
The maximal radius is obtained when $B \ge C \ge A$.

For the triangle at hand, $r$ is maximized by $b = 4$ and $A = 90^\circ$, which gives $r = 4$, and $s$ by $b = 5$ and $A$ the angle opposite the side of length $3$. Then $\tan A = \frac{3}{4}$, $\tan \frac{A}{2} = \frac{1}{3}$, which produces the greatest radius $s = 15$, which is thus the answer.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先固定圆所切的两条边，按圆心落在相应角的内角平分线或外角平分线分类。把半径表示成一条边与半角函数的乘积，再比较所有顶点安排。
2. 若圆切 $AB,AC$ 且过 $C$，内角平分线情形有 $r=b\tan(A/2)$，外角平分线情形有 $s=b/\tan(A/2)$。利用 $\sin x/\tan(x/2)=2\cos^2(x/2)$ 的单调性确定各情形应如何排列边和对角。
3. 内角情形取直角所对的安排并代入 $b=4$；外角情形取 $b=5$、令 $A$ 对边长为 $3$，由 $\tan A=3/4$ 求出 $\tan(A/2)$，再代入 $s=b/\tan(A/2)$ 比较。
