# G-060｜Right-Angled Pentagon Angle Correspondence

## 题面

Problem:

Let $ABCDE$ be a convex pentagon with $CD = DE$ and $\angle BCD = \angle DEA = 90^{\circ}$. Point $F$ lies on $AB$ such that $\frac{AF}{AE} = \frac{BF}{BC}$. Prove that $\angle FCE = \angle ADE$ and $\angle FEC = \angle BDC$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:

Let $\omega$ denote the circumcircle of $\triangle CDE$ and let $D_{1}$ be the point opposite to $D$. Let $DA$ meet $\omega$ at $A_{1}$ and let $F' = A_{1}C \cap AB$. If we let $\alpha = \angle DA_{1}C = \angle DD_{1}C = \angle ED_{1}D$ then
$$
\frac{AF'}{AE} = \frac{AF' \cdot AD_{1}}{AA_{1} \cdot AD} = \frac{\sin \alpha \cdot \sin \angle D_{1}DA}{\sin \angle A_{1}F'A \cdot \sin \alpha} = \frac{\sin \angle D_{1}CA}{\sin \angle CF'B} = \frac{F'B}{BC}
$$
Hence $F = F'$. Now $\angle F'CE = \angle A_{1}CE = \angle A_{1}DE = \angle ADE$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 不要直接追点 $F$ 的角；先在 $\triangle CDE$ 的外接圆上构造一个候选点 $F'$，再用题设比例证明它就是 $F$。
2. 设 $D_1$ 为圆上与 $D$ 相对的点，$DA$ 再交圆于 $A_1$，并令 $F'=A_1C\cap AB$。围绕 $A_1,C,D,E,D_1$ 的圆周角，把 $AF'/AE$ 转成可与 $F'B/BC$ 比较的正弦比。
3. 按解法中的角记号 $\alpha=\angle DA_1C=\angle DD_1C=\angle ED_1D$ 整理正弦比，先得到 $AF'/AE=F'B/BC$，从而识别 $F'$。随后沿 $A_1,C,D,E$ 的同圆关系转移所需角，并结合题设两处直角追完剩余角。
