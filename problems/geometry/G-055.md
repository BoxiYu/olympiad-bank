# G-055｜Square Tiling by 30-60-90 Triangles

## 题面

Problem:
Is it possible to cover a given square with a few congruent right-angled triangles with acute angle equal to $30^{\circ}$? (The triangles may not overlap and may not exceed the margins of the square.)

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:
We will prove that desired covering is impossible.
Let us assume the opposite, i.e., a square with side length $a$ can be tiled with $k$ congruent right-angled triangles, whose sides are of lengths $b$, $b \sqrt{3}$, and $2b$.

Then the area of such a triangle is $\frac{b^{2} \sqrt{3}}{2}$.
And the area of the square is
$$
S_{sq} = k b^{2} \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$
Furthermore, the length of the side of the square, $a$, is obtained by the contribution of an integer number of lengths $b$, $2b$, and $b \sqrt{3}$, hence
$$
a = m b \sqrt{3} + n b
$$
where $m, n \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\}$, and at least one of the numbers $m$ and $n$ is different from zero. So the area of the square is
$$
S_{sq} = a^{2} = (m b \sqrt{3} + n b)^{2} = b^{2} \left(3 m^{2} + n^{2} + 2 \sqrt{3} m n\right)
$$
Now because of (1) and (2) it follows $3 m^{2} + n^{2} + 2 \sqrt{3} m n = k \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, i.e.
$$
6 m^{2} + 2 n^{2} = (k - 4 m n) \sqrt{3}
$$
Because $3 m^{2} + n^{2} \neq 0$ and from the equality (3) it follows $4 m n \neq k$. Using once more (3), we get
$$
\sqrt{3} = \frac{6 m^{2} + 2 n^{2}}{k - 4 m n}
$$
which contradicts the fact that $\sqrt{3}$ is irrational, because $\frac{6 m^{2} + 2 n^{2}}{k - 4 m n}$ is a rational number.

Finally, we have obtained a contradiction, which proves that the desired covering is impossible.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先假设这样的铺法存在，同时从小三角形面积总和与正方形边界拼接两方面描述正方形。留意两种描述中 $\sqrt3$ 的地位。
2. 设小三角形三边为 $b,b\sqrt3,2b$。正方形一边必可写成 $mb\sqrt3+nb$，而其面积也等于若干个小三角形面积之和。
3. 将两种面积表达式相等并整理，把 $\sqrt3$ 单独写成只含整数 $m,n,k$ 的分式；再由 $m,n$ 不全为零检查分母不能为零。
