# G-066｜Tangent-Circle Concurrency

## 题面

Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrangle and let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the sides $AD$ and $BC$, respectively. The circle through $A$ and $D$ tangent to $AC$ crosses the line $AB$ again at $P$ and the circle through $B$ and $C$ tangent to $BD$ crosses the line $AB$ again at $Q$. Let $t_A$ be the tangent of the circle $AMP$ at $A$ and let $t_B$ be the tangent of the circle $BNQ$ at $B$. Prove that the lines $CD$, $t_A$ and $t_B$ are concurrent.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

We first prove that the triangles $ADP$ and $CDB$ are similar. Since $ABCD$ is cyclic, $\angle DAP = \angle DCB$; and since $AC$ is tangent to the circle $ADP$, $\angle CBD = \angle CAD = \angle APD$. Consequently, the triangles $ADP$ and $CDB$ are similar.

Let $R$ be the midpoint of $CD$. The points $M$ and $R$ correspond under the above similarity, so $\angle PMA = \angle BRC$.

Let the line $CD$ meet the circle $ABR$ again at $K$ (possibly, $K = R$). Notice that $\angle BAK = \angle BRC = \angle PMA$, so $AK$ is tangent to the circle $AMP$.

Similarly, $BK$ is tangent to the circle $BNQ$ and the conclusion follows.

Alternative solution.
We prove the conclusion under the weaker assumptions that $ABCD$ is merely convex and $AM/DM = CN/BN$.

Let $AB$ and $CD$ meet at $E$; if the two are parallel, then $E$ is their common ideal point. Let $CD$ cross $t_A$ and $t_B$ at $K_A$ and $K_B$, respectively. We will show that $K_A = K_B$, whence the conclusion.

Let $I$ and $J$ be the ideal points of $AD$ and $BC$, respectively. The line pencils
($AD, AC, AE, AK_A$) and ($PA, PD, PI, PM$)
are congruent, since $\angle K_A AD = \angle APM$, $\angle CAD = \angle APD$ and $\angle IAE = \angle IPE$. Hence $(D, C; E, K_A) = (AD, AC; AE, AK_A) = (PA, PD; PI, PM) = (A, D; I, M) = DM/AM$. Similarly, $(D, C; E, K_B) = (BD, BC; BE, BK_B) = (QC, QB; QJ, QN) = (C, B; J, N) = BN/CN$.

Finally, recall the assumption $AM/DM = CN/BN$. By the preceding, $(D, C; E, K_A) = (D, C; E, K_B)$, so $K_A = K_B$, as desired. This ends the proof.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先把第一个切圆提供的角与原圆内接角联系起来，寻找 △ADP 和由 C、D、B 组成的三角形之间的相似。第二个切圆稍后完全对称处理。
2. 设 R 为 CD 的中点。上述相似使 M 与 R 对应，因此得到 ∠PMA=∠BRC。
3. 令直线 CD 与 △ABR 的外接圆再交于 K，则 ∠BAK=∠BRC=∠PMA，故 AK 是圆 AMP 在 A 处的切线。对另一侧作同样论证，得到 BK 是圆 BNQ 在 B 处的切线。
