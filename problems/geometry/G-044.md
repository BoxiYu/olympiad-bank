# G-044｜Cube in a Spherical Gap

## 题面

Problem:
A cube with side length $2$ is inscribed in a sphere. A second cube, with faces parallel to the first, is inscribed between the sphere and one face of the first cube. What is the length of a side of the smaller cube?

## 答案

2/3

## 解法要点

Solution:
First note that the long diagonal of the cube has length $2\sqrt{3}$, so the radius of the sphere is $\sqrt{3}$. Let $x$ be the side length of the smaller cube. Then the distance from the center of the sphere to the far face of the smaller cube is $1 + x$, while the distance from the center of the far face to a vertex lying on the sphere is $\frac{x\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Therefore, the square of the radius is
$$
3 = (1 + x)^2 + \frac{x^2}{2},
$$
or
$$
3x^2 + 4x - 4 = (3x - 2)(x + 2) = 0,
$$
so $x = \frac{2}{3}$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 取过球心且垂直于两立方体对应面的截面，关注小立方体最远面上的顶点到球心的距离。
2. 大立方体的体对角线给出球半径；若小立方体边长为 x，球心到其远面的垂距与该面中心到顶点的距离互相垂直。
3. 写出半径平方等于 (1+x)² 与 x²/2 之和，整理所得二次方程并只保留正根。
