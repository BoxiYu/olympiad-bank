# G-017｜Distance Between Derived Circumcenters

## 题面

Problem:

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB = 13$, $BC = 14$, $CA = 15$. Let $O$ be the circumcenter of $ABC$. Find the distance between the circumcenters of triangles $AOB$ and $AOC$.

## 答案

91/6

## 解法要点

Solution:

Let $S$, $T$ be the intersections of the tangents to the circumcircle of $ABC$ at $A$, $C$ and at $A$, $B$ respectively. Note that $ASCO$ is cyclic with diameter $SO$, so the circumcenter of $AOC$ is the midpoint of $OS$, and similarly for the other side. So the length we want is $\frac{1}{2} ST$. The circumradius $R$ of $ABC$ can be computed by Heron's formula and $K = \frac{abc}{4R}$, giving $R = \frac{65}{8}$. A few applications of the Pythagorean theorem and similar triangles gives $AT = \frac{65}{6}$, $AS = \frac{39}{2}$, so the answer is $\frac{91}{6}$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把两个待求圆心与原外接圆的切线交点联系起来，使所求距离变成一条更易计算的线段的一半。
2. 设原外接圆在 $A,C$ 处的切线交于 $S$，在 $A,B$ 处的切线交于 $T$；四边形 $ASCO$ 以 $SO$ 为直径共圆，所以 $\triangle AOC$ 的圆心是 $OS$ 中点，另一边同理。
3. 因此只需求 $ST$。先用海伦公式和 $K=abc/(4R)$ 求原三角形外接半径，再用勾股与相似关系得到 $AT=65/6、AS=39/2$，据此求出 $ST$ 后取一半。
