# G-008｜Four Pedal Points Concyclic

## 题面

Let $ABC$ be an arbitrary triangle. A circle passes through $B$ and $C$ and intersects the lines $AB$ and $AC$ in $D$ and $E$, respectively. The projections of the points $B$ and $E$ on $CD$ are denoted by $B'$ and $E'$, respectively. The projections of the points $D$ and $C$ on $BE$ are denoted by $D'$ and $C'$, respectively.

Prove that the points $B'$, $D'$, $E'$ and $C'$ lie on the same circle.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Let $I$ be the intersection point of the lines $BE$ and $CD$. The quadrilaterals $BD'B'D$ and $CE'C'E$ are cyclic, so $\overline{BDB'} = \overline{B'D'I}$ and $\overline{CEC'} = \overline{IE'C'}$. Since $BDEC$ is also cyclic, $\overline{BDB'} = \overline{CEC'}$. It follows that $\overline{B'D'I} = \overline{IE'C'}$, so $B'D'E'C'$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.

---

Using the power of a point theorem, one has:
$$
IB' \cdot ID = ID' \cdot IB
$$
$$
IC' \cdot IE = IE' \cdot IC
$$
$$
IE \cdot IB = ID \cdot IC
$$
From these one easily obtains
$$
IB' \cdot IE' = ID' \cdot IC'
$$
which proves that the quadrilateral $B'D'E'C'$ is cyclic, using the reciprocal of the power of a point theorem.

## 提示阶梯

1. 引入直线 $BE$ 与 $CD$ 的交点，把四个投影产生的直角与原来的圆联系起来。
2. 设 $I=BE\cap CD$；投影关系分别给出两个含直角的圆内接四边形 $BD'B'D$ 与 $CE'C'E$。
3. 从这两个圆内接四边形把目标角分别转移到 $\angle BDB'$ 与 $\angle CEC'$；再利用 $B,D,E,C$ 共圆说明这两个角相等。
