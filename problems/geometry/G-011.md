# G-011｜Opposite Triangle Incenters

## 题面

Problem:

Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral. Let $I_{A}$ be the incenter of $BCD$ and define $I_{B}, I_{C}, I_{D}$ similarly. Show that $AC \perp I_{B}I_{D}$ if and only if $I_{A}I_{C} \perp BD$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:

We show that both are true if and only if $AB + CD = BC + DA$.

Let's first consider the triangle $ABD$. We know the incircle of $ABD$ is centered at $I_{C}$. Let $X, Y, T_{A}$ denote the points where the incircle is tangent to $AB$, $AD$, and $BD$, respectively. We have that $AX = AY$, $BX = BT_{A}$, and $DT_{A} = DY$ since each of these are segments tangent to the incircle from a common point. Thus,
$$
AB - AD = (AX + XB) - (AY + YD) = BX - CY = BT_{A} - DT_{A}.
$$
We now include the triangle $BCD$. Let $T_{C}$ denote the point at which the incircle of $BCD$, centered at $I_{A}$, is tangent to $BD$. Replicating the argument for $ABD$, we see that $BT_{C} - DT_{C} = BC - CD$. Now, $I_{A}I_{C} \perp BD$ iff $T_{A} = T_{C}$, since the altitudes from $I_{A}$ and $I_{C}$ to diagonal $BD$ must meet at a point. Hence, $I_{A}I_{C} \perp BD$ is equivalent to
$$
BA - DA = BT_{A} - DT_{A} = BT_{C} - DT_{C} = BC - CD,
$$
which in turn is equivalent to $AB + CD = AD + BC$.

We use a symmetric argument, but this time considering triangles $ABC$ and $ABD$, to show that a necessary and sufficient condition for $I_{B}I_{D} \perp AC$ is also $AB + CD = AD + BC$. Since $AC \perp I_{B}I_{D}$ and $I_{A}I_{C} \perp BD$ are both equivalent to $AB + CD = AD + BC$, we have $AC \perp I_{B}I_{D} \Longleftrightarrow I_{A}I_{C} \perp BD$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把两个垂直条件都转化为同一条边长关系；切入点是各内切圆在两条对角线上的切点。
2. 在共用对角线 $BD$ 的两个三角形中，两内心连线垂直于 $BD$，恰好等价于它们在 $BD$ 上的垂足（也就是切点）重合。
3. 利用同一点引圆的两条切线段相等，把切点到 $B,D$ 的有向差分别化为 $AB-AD$ 与 $BC-CD$，从而得到共同条件 $AB+CD=AD+BC$；对另一条对角线作对称论证。
