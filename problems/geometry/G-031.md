# G-031｜Rotated Interior Point Distance Sum

## 题面

Problem:

Let triangle $ABC$ be such that $AB = AC = 22$ and $BC = 11$. Point $D$ is chosen in the interior of the triangle such that $AD = 19$ and $\angle ABD + \angle ACD = 90^\circ$. The value of $BD^2 + CD^2$ can be expressed as $\frac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $100a + b$.

## 答案

36104

## 解法要点

Solution:

Rotate triangle $ABD$ about $A$ so that $B$ coincides with $C$. Let $D$ map to $D'$ under this. Note that $CDD'$ is a right triangle with right angle at $C$. Also, note that $ADD'$ is similar to $ABC$. Thus, we have $DD' = \frac{AD}{2} = \frac{19}{2}$. Finally, note that
$$
BD^2 + CD^2 = CD'^2 + CD^2 = DD'^2 = \frac{361}{4}
$$

## 提示阶梯

1. 题设给了等腰结构和两个角之和，适合绕顶点 $A$ 旋转，把 $BD$ 搬到以 $C$ 为端点的线段。
2. 将三角形 $ABD$ 绕 $A$ 旋转到 $B$ 与 $C$ 重合，设 $D$ 的像为 $D'$。角度条件会使三角形 $CDD'$ 成为直角三角形。
3. 旋转给出 $CD'=BD$；再由 $\triangle ADD'\sim\triangle ABC$ 求出 $DD'$ 与 $AD$ 的比例。于是把 $BD^2+CD^2$ 改写为直角三角形 $CDD'$ 的两直角边平方和。
