# G-036｜Right-Triangle Ratio Inequality

## 题面

Let $a$ and $b$ be the legs, and $c$ the hypotenuse of a right triangle.
Prove that the following inequality holds
$$
\left(1 + \frac{c}{a}\right) \left(1 + \frac{c}{b}\right) \geqslant 3 + 2\sqrt{2}.
$$

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

By rearranging the left hand side of the inequality we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(1 + \frac{c}{a}\right) \left(1 + \frac{c}{b}\right) &= \frac{(a+c)(b+c)}{ab} = \frac{c^2 + ab + c(a+b)}{ab} \\
&= \frac{c^2}{ab} + 1 + \frac{c(a+b)}{ab}
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the triangle is right it follows that $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
$$
= \frac{a^2 + b^2}{ab} + 1 + \frac{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}(a+b)}{ab}
$$

Now we use $a^2 + b^2 \ge 2ab$ and $a + b \ge 2\sqrt{ab}$ to get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\ge & \frac{2ab}{ab} + 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2ab} \cdot 2\sqrt{ab}}{ab} \\
= & 2 + 1 + 2\sqrt{2} = 3 + 2\sqrt{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

## 提示阶梯

1. 先展开左式，并用直角三角形关系消去斜边；目标是把各项化成适合直接估下界的对称形式。
2. 将左式整理为 $c^2/(ab)+1+c(a+b)/(ab)$，再代入 $c^2=a^2+b^2$。随后分别控制 $a^2+b^2$ 与 $a+b$。
3. 同时使用 $a^2+b^2\ge 2ab$ 和 $a+b\ge2\sqrt{ab}$；在含 $c(a+b)$ 的项中还要用 $c=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\ge\sqrt{2ab}$，逐项代入后合并。
