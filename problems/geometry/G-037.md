# G-037｜Right Triangle from Inradius and Leg

## 题面

Problem:

Right triangle $X Y Z$, with hypotenuse $Y Z$, has an incircle of radius $\frac{3}{8}$ and one leg of length $3$. Find the area of the triangle.

## 答案

21/16

## 解法要点

Solution:

Let the other leg have length $x$. Then the tangents from $Y$ and $Z$ to the incircle have length $x - \frac{3}{8}$ and $3 - \frac{3}{8}$. So the hypotenuse has length $x + \frac{9}{4}$, the semiperimeter of the triangle is $x + \frac{21}{8}$, and the area of the triangle is $\frac{3}{8}\left(x + \frac{21}{8}\right)$. But the area can also be calculated as $\frac{3x}{2}$. Setting these expressions equal, we find $x = \frac{7}{8}$ and the area is $\frac{21}{16}$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 设未知直角边为 $x$，通过内切圆切点把斜边和半周长都写成 $x$ 的一次式，再从两种面积公式建立方程。
2. 同一点引圆的两条切线等长；直角顶点到两个切点的长度都等于内切圆半径。依次表示另外两个顶点的切线段。
3. 把斜边写成两段切线长之和，并得到半周长 $s$。然后令 $rs$ 与两直角边乘积的一半相等，解出 $x$ 后代回任一面积式。
