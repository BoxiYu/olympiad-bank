# G-012｜Parallel Incircle Tangent Inequality

## 题面

Let $k$ be the incircle of a $\triangle ABC$. A line, parallel to $BC$ touches $k$ and intersects the sides $AB$ and $AC$ at points $A_1$ and $A_2$. Define the points $B_1, B_2$ and $C_1, C_2$ in a similar way. Prove that
$$
9(\overline{AA_1} \cdot \overline{AA_2} + \overline{BB_1} \cdot \overline{BB_2} + \overline{CC_1} \cdot \overline{CC_2}) \ge \overline{AB}^2 + \overline{BC}^2 + \overline{CA}^2.
$$

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Let $D, E$ and $F$ be the common points of with the sides $BC, CA$ and $AB$, respectively, and let $AE = AF = x$, $BF = BD = y$ and $CD = CE = z$.
Since $\triangle AA_1A_2 \sim \triangle ABC$, then $\frac{AA_1}{AB} = \frac{AA_2}{AC} = \frac{P_{AA_1A_2}}{P_{ABC}} = \frac{x}{x+y+z}$ and hence $AA_1 = \frac{x(x+y)}{x+y+z}$ and $AA_2 = \frac{x(x+z)}{x+y+z}$. Analogously, $BB_1 = \frac{y(y+z)}{x+y+z}$, $BB_2 = \frac{y(y+x)}{x+y+z}$, $CC_1 = \frac{z(z+x)}{x+y+z}$ and $CC_2 = \frac{z(z+y)}{x+y+z}$.
Then the given inequality is equivalent to
$$
9 \sum x^2 (x+y)(x+z) \ge (x+y+z)^2 \sum (x+y)^2,
$$
i.e.
$$
9 \sum x^4 + 2(\sum x^2)(\sum xy) \ge 2(\sum x^2)^2 + 4(\sum xy)^2,
$$

The last inequality follows by the well-known inequalities
$$
3(x^4 + y^4 + z^4) \geq (x^2 + y^2 + z^2)^2 \text{ and } x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \geq xy + yz + zx.
$$

## 提示阶梯

1. 先用三边上的内切圆切线段作变量，把六条待估线段统一写成这三个变量的式子。
2. 设从 $A,B,C$ 引出的相等切线段分别为 $x,y,z$；由小三角形与 $ABC$ 相似，顶点 $A$ 对应的两段比例都是 $x/(x+y+z)$，其余循环类推。
3. 代换并清分母后，把式子整理为关于 $\sum x^4$、$\sum x^2$、$\sum xy$ 的对称式；分别使用 $3\sum x^4\ge(\sum x^2)^2$ 与 $\sum x^2\ge\sum xy$ 完成比较。
