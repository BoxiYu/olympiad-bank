# G-042｜Reflections and Tangent Circumcircle

## 题面

Problem:

Triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle $\omega$ such that $\angle A = 60^\circ$ and $\angle B = 75^\circ$. Let the bisector of angle $A$ meet $BC$ and $\omega$ at $E$ and $D$, respectively. Let the reflections of $A$ across $D$ and $C$ be $D'$ and $C'$, respectively. If the tangent to $\omega$ at $A$ meets line $BC$ at $P$, and the circumcircle of $APD'$ meets line $AC$ at $F \neq A$, prove that the circumcircle of $C'FE$ is tangent to $BC$ at $E$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Solution:

We will show that $CE^2 = (CF)(CC')$. By a simple computation using the given angles, one may find that this is equivalent to $CF = AC - AB$, or $AF = 2AC - AB$.

We compute $AF$ by trigonometry. Assume for simplicity that $AC = \frac{1}{2}$, so $AD' = 2AD = 2AC = 1$ because $\triangle ACD$ is isosceles by angle chasing. We first compute $PD'$ by the law of cosines on triangle $APD'$, which yields
$$
PD'^2 = AP^2 + AD'^2 - 2(AP)(AD') \cos 75 = AP^2 + 1 - 2AP \cos 75.
$$
We may easily compute $AP$ by the law of sines in triangle $BAP$ to be $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. Thus,
$$
PD'^2 = \frac{1}{2} + 1 - \sqrt{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{4 - \sqrt{3}}{2}.
$$
By the law of sines within the circumcircle of $APD'F$, we have
$$
\frac{FD'}{\sin 30} = \frac{PD'}{\sin 75}.
$$
From this, we find that
$$
FD' = \frac{PD' \sin 30}{\sin 75} = \frac{4 - \sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}.
$$
Thus, we have by the law of cosines on triangle $D'AF$ that
$$
AF^2 + AD'^2 - 2(AF)(AD') \cos 30 = AF^2 + 1 - \sqrt{3} AF = FD'^2 = \frac{4 - \sqrt{3}}{4 + 2\sqrt{3}}.
$$
Finally, solving for $AF$ yields
$$
AF = \frac{\sqrt{3} \pm (2\sqrt{3} - 3)}{2}
$$
from which we indeed get $AF = \frac{3 - \sqrt{3}}{2}$ which equals $2AC - AB$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把圆在 $E$ 处与 $BC$ 相切转化为幂的等式；由于 $C'$ 是 $C$ 关于 $A$ 的对称点，关键只剩确定 $F$ 在 $AC$ 上的位置。
2. 目标可化为 $CE^2=CF\cdot CC'$，而角度计算又把它化成关于 $AF,AC,AB$ 的线性关系。接下来只需在圆 $APD'F$ 中求出 $AF$。
3. 可先归一化 $AC$，由 $\triangle ACD$ 的角关系求 $AD'$，再在 $\triangle APD'$ 中用余弦定理求 $PD'$；利用 $A,P,D',F$ 共圆的正弦关系求 $FD'$，最后在 $\triangle AD'F$ 中用余弦定理解出符合点位的 $AF$ 分支。
