# G-022｜Concurrent Cevian Ratio Sum

## 题面

Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $X$, $Y$, $Z$ be points on $BC$, $CA$, $AB$, respectively. Suppose that $AX$, $BY$ and $CZ$ intersect in a point $P$. Prove that
$$
\frac{AP}{AX} + \frac{BP}{BY} + \frac{CP}{CZ} = 2.
$$

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Assign weights $p, q, r$ to the vertices $A, B, C$, respectively, in such a way that the centre of mass is $P$. Draw the line $L$ through $P$, parallel to $BC$. Let $d$ denote the distance of $A$ to $L$, and let $h$ denote the distance of $A$ to $BC$. Since the triangle will be in static equilibrium when balanced (horizontally) upon $L$, we infer that $pd = (q+r)(h-d)$. This leads to
$$
\frac{p}{q+r} = \frac{h-d}{d} = \frac{AX-AP}{AP},
$$
which is equivalent to
$$
\frac{AP}{AX} = \frac{q+r}{p+q+r}.
$$
Since one may similarly derive
$$
\frac{BP}{BY} = \frac{r+p}{p+q+r} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{CP}{CZ} = \frac{p+q}{p+q+r},
$$
the desired conclusion follows by summation.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把交点 $P$ 看成三角形顶点带权后的重心，尝试把每个线段比解释为某两组权重之和的占比。
2. 给 $A,B,C$ 分别赋权 $p,q,r$。过 $P$ 作一条平行于一边的直线，用两侧权重关于该直线的平衡关系连接高度比与 $AP/AX$。
3. 若过 $P$ 的平行线到 $A$、$BC$ 的距离分别为 $d$、$h-d$，写出 $pd=(q+r)(h-d)$，从而化出 $AP/AX=(q+r)/(p+q+r)$；其余两式循环得到后相加。
