# G-028｜Excircle and MPQ Circle Tangency

## 题面

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle and let $\omega$ be its $A$-excircle (excircle opposite vertex $A$). Let $D, E, F$ be the points where $\omega$ touches the lines $BC, CA, AB$, respectively. The circle $AEF$ crosses the line $BC$ at $P$ and $Q$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the line segment $AD$. Prove that the circles $\omega$ and $MPQ$ are tangent.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

For convenience, let $\Omega$ and $\gamma$ denote the circles $AEFPQ$ and $MPQ$, respectively. Letting the line $AD$ cross $\omega$ again at $T$, we will show that $\gamma$ and $\omega$ are tangent at $T$.

We first prove that the points $M, P, Q, T$ are concyclic, so $T$ lies on $\gamma$. Let $A'$ be the center of $\omega$, and notice that $AA'$ is a diameter of $\Omega$, since $A'E$ and $A'F$ are perpendicular to $AE$ and $AF$, respectively. Let $N$ be the midpoint of the line segment $DT$. Since $A'D = A'T$, the angle $A'NA$ is right, so $N$ also lies on $\Omega$, and therefore $DP \cdot DQ = DA \cdot DN$; and since $DA = 2DM$ and $DT = 2DN$, it follows that $DP \cdot DQ = DM \cdot DT$, showing that $M, P, Q, T$ are indeed concyclic.

If $EF$ and $BC$ are parallel, then the triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with apex at $A$ and the conclusion follows by symmetry.

Otherwise, the lines $PQ$ and $EF$ cross at a point $R$; these lines are the radical axes of $\Omega, \gamma$ and $\Omega, \omega$, respectively, so $R$ is the radical center of the three circles, and $RT$ is the radical axis of $\gamma$ and $\omega$.

To conclude the proof, it is therefore sufficient to show that $RT$ is tangent to $\omega$. With respect to this circle, the line $DR$ is the polar of $D$, and the line $EF$ is the polar of $A$, so $R = DR \cap EF$ is the pole of the line $ADT$. Consequently, $RT$ is indeed tangent to $\omega$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 先猜出两圆可能的切点：让 $AD$ 再交旁切圆于 $T$，并设法证明 $T$ 也在圆 $MPQ$ 上。
2. 记 $AEFPQ$ 的圆为 $\Omega$，旁切圆圆心为 $A'$；$AA'$ 是 $\Omega$ 的直径。取 $N$ 为 $DT$ 中点，可借幂关系把 $DP\cdot DQ$ 转成 $DM\cdot DT$。
3. 证明 $M,P,Q,T$ 共圆后，令 $R=PQ\cap EF$；$R$ 是三个圆的根心，所以两目标圆的根轴是 $RT$。再用 $DR$、$EF$ 分别是旁切圆关于 $D$、$A$ 的极线，判定 $R$ 为直线 $ADT$ 的极点。
