# G-006｜Maximum Dragoon Firing Formation

## 题面

Problem:

Dragoons take up $1 \times 1$ squares in the plane with sides parallel to the coordinate axes such that the interiors of the squares do not intersect. A dragoon can fire at another dragoon if the difference in the $x$-coordinates of their centers and the difference in the $y$-coordinates of their centers are both at most $6$, regardless of any dragoons in between. For example, a dragoon centered at $(4,5)$ can fire at a dragoon centered at the origin, but a dragoon centered at $(7,0)$ can not. A dragoon cannot fire at itself. What is the maximum number of dragoons that can fire at a single dragoon simultaneously?

## 答案

168

## 解法要点

Solution:

Assign coordinates in such a way that the dragoon being fired on is centered at $(0,0)$. Any dragoon firing at it must have a center with $x$-coordinates and $y$-coordinates that are no smaller than $-6$ and no greater than $6$. That means that every dragoon firing at it must lie entirely in the region bounded by the lines $x=-6.5$, $x=6.5$, $y=-6.5$, and $y=6.5$. This is a square with sides of length $13$, so there is room for exactly $169$ dragoons in it. One of them is the dragoon being fired on, so there are at most $168$ dragoons firing at it.

## 提示阶梯

1. 固定被射击者的中心，考察所有射击者的中心及其单位正方形共同落在哪个区域内。
2. 中心的两个坐标都被限制在同一个区间内；把单位正方形的半边长也计入后，所有相关正方形都装在一个边长为 $13$ 的正方形中。
3. 用 $1\times1$ 方格恰好铺满这个大正方形可给出容量上界；最后记得其中一个位置已由被射击者自身占据。
