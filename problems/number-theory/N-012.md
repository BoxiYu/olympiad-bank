# N-012｜Divisibility Adjacencies in Permutations

## 题面

Problem:

In a sequence of numbers, a term is called golden if it is divisible by the term immediately before it. What is the maximum possible number of golden terms in a permutation of $1,2,3,\ldots ,2021$?

## 答案

1010

## 解法要点

Solution:

Let $k$ be the number of golden terms. We claim that $k \leq 1010$.

Proof: Define the term immediately before a golden term to be a silver term. The number of silver terms is also $k$. If $a$ is any silver term and $b$ is the corresponding golden term then we must have
$$a \leq \frac{b}{2} \leq \frac{2021}{2} < 1011.$$  
Therefore $a \leq 1010$. Hence $a \in \{1,2,3,\ldots ,1010\}$ and therefore there can be at most 1010 different silver terms. Therefore $k \leq 1010$.

Now we need to show that $k = 1010$ is indeed possible. To do this we first partition the set $\{1,2,3,\ldots ,2021\}$ into the following distinct parts:
$$\{1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,256,512,1024\},$$
$$\{3,6,12,24,48,96,192,384,768,1536\},$$
$$\{5,10,20,40,80,160,320,640,1280\},$$
$$\{7,14,28,56,112,224,448,896,1792\},$$
$$\ldots$$
$$\{2019\},$$
$$\{2021\}.$$  
Each part starts with an odd number, which is then doubled until the result is larger than 2021.

Putting each part in the above sequence in order is our example. In this example every even term, $2n$, is golden because it occurs immediately after the term $n$. There are 1010 even numbers, so $k = 1010$ is possible.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把每个 golden 项与它前面的项配成一对，先寻找前项必须满足的大小限制；随后再考虑怎样让大量整除关系同时出现。
2. 若 b 是 golden 项、a 是其前项，则 a 是 b 的真因子，所以 a≤b/2。构造方面，可把所有数按“奇数部分相同”分成不断倍增的链。
3. 上界可由所有 silver 项都落在不超过 2021/2 的整数中得到；把每条倍增链按递增顺序首尾相接，则链内除首项外的每个偶数都会贡献一次。
