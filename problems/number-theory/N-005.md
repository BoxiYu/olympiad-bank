# N-005｜Divisors Separated by Square Root

## 题面

Show that there are infinitely many positive integer numbers $n$ such that $n^2 + 1$ has two positive divisors whose difference is $n$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Define the sequence $(a_k)_{k \ge 0}$ by $a_0 = 1$, $a_1 = 2$ and $a_{k+2} a_k = a_{k+1}^2 + 1$, $k = 0, 1, 2, \dots$, and check inductively that the $a_k$ are all positive integer numbers, the $n_k = a_{k+1} - a_k$ form a strictly increasing sequence of positive integer numbers, and $a_k$ and $a_{k+1}$ both divide $n_k^2 + 1$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 尝试递归构造一对相邻整数，让它们同时整除由两者之差生成的平方加一。
2. 从 $a_0=1,a_1=2$ 出发，规定 $a_{k+2}a_k=a_{k+1}^2+1$，并令候选数为 $n_k=a_{k+1}-a_k$。
3. 归纳验证每个 $a_k$ 都是正整数，且 $a_k,a_{k+1}$ 均整除 $n_k^2+1$；最后证明这些差严格递增，再据此收尾。
