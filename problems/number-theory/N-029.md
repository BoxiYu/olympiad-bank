# N-029｜Power Recurrence Avoiding a Repeated-Block Integer

## 题面

Suppose $(a_n)$ is a sequence of integers such that $a_1$ and $a_2$ are two distinct digits of the number $2014$, and $a_n = a_{n-1}^{10} + a_{n-2}^{10}$ for all $n > 2$.
Let $m = 20142014\ldots2014$ be the number given by the string $2014$ repeated $2014$ times. Show that $m$ cannot be $a_n$ for any positive integer $n$.

## 答案

证明题

## 解法要点

Note first that
$$
\begin{align*}
a_3 &= a_1^{10} + a_2^{10} \le 2^{10} + 4^{10} < 2 \cdot 4^{10} = 2^{21} < 10^{21} < m \quad \text{and} \\
a_4 &= a_2^{10} + a_3^{10} < 4^{10} + 10^{20} < 2 \cdot 10^{20} < m.
\end{align*}
$$
Hence, if $m = a_n$, then $n \ge 5$.
Given the possible values for $a_1$ and $a_2$, we see that $a_3$ is congruent to either $1$ or $2$ modulo $3$, depending on whether or not we select $0$ as one of the two initial terms of the sequence. Similarly, $a_4$ is congruent to either $1$ or $2$ modulo $3$, depending on whether or not $a_2 = 0$. In any case, neither $a_3$ nor $a_4$ is divisible by $3$.
By the Little Theorem of Fermat, $a_k^{10} \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$ unless $a_k \equiv 0 \pmod{3}$.
Therefore, if neither $a_{k-1}$ nor $a_{k-2}$ is divisible by $3$, then
$$
a_k = a_{k-1}^{10} + a_{k-2}^{10} \equiv 2 \pmod{3}.
$$
By induction it follows now that $a_n \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$ for all $n \ge 5$.
Finally, using that any integer is congruent to its sum of digits modulo $3$, we find that
$$
m \equiv 2014 \cdot (2 + 0 + 1 + 4) \equiv 2014 \equiv 1 \pmod{3}
$$
and we conclude that $m \neq a_n$ for all $n \ge 1$.

## 提示阶梯

1. 不要比较递推项的完整大小；先排除最初几项，再寻找一个能让十次幂大幅简化的小模数。
2. 模 3 考察：非零余数的十次幂都同余于 1。先检查第三、第四项均不被 3 整除，再从递推式归纳后续余数。
3. 由连续两项都非零模 3，可推出下一项固定同余于 2，并保持这一状态；最后用十进制数与其数字和模 3 同余来比较目标数。
