# N-022｜Minimum Denominator of a Rational Sum

## 题面

Problem:

The rational numbers $x$ and $y$, when written in lowest terms, have denominators $60$ and $70$, respectively. What is the smallest possible denominator of $x+y$?

## 答案

84

## 解法要点

Solution:

Write $x + y = \dfrac{a}{60} + \dfrac{b}{70} = \dfrac{7a + 6b}{420}$. Since $a$ is relatively prime to $60$ and $b$ is relatively prime to $70$, it follows that none of the primes $2, 3, 7$ can divide $7a + 6b$, so we won't be able to cancel any of these factors in the denominator. Thus, after reducing to lowest terms, the denominator will still be at least $2^{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 7 = 84$ (the product of the powers of $2, 3$, and $7$ dividing $420$). On the other hand, $84$ is achievable, by taking (e.g.) $\dfrac{1}{60} + \dfrac{3}{70} = \dfrac{25}{420} = \dfrac{5}{84}$. So $84$ is the answer.

## 提示阶梯

1. 把两个最简分数通分，问题变成分子与公共分母最多能约去哪些素因子。最简条件会限制原分子的整除性。
2. 写 x=a/60、y=b/70，则和的分子为 7a+6b，公共分母为 420。分别模 2、3、7 检查这个分子为何不能含这些素因子。
3. 下界由 420 中无法约掉的 2、3、7 的幂次给出。为说明可达到，可选满足各自互素条件的 a、b，使 7a+6b 与 420 的最大公因数恰好只含剩余的素因子。
